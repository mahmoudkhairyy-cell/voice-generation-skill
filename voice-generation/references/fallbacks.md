# Fallbacks (pick ONE)

1. **Record your own voice in CapCut (most reliable, always free).** Claude gives the numbered script. In CapCut: new project, Audio, Record, read one line at a time. Natural Khaleeji or Egyptian, no dialect risk.
2. **Another free web TTS.** TTSMaker (ttsmaker.com) has Arabic voices and states that its audio can be used commercially, but its public page was not reachable by automation, so it was not tested end to end. Voices are labelled by country (Saudi Arabia, UAE and so on). Audio links are deleted after about 30 minutes, so download at once.
3. **Edge "Read aloud"** only to hear the script and proofread it. It does not save a file.

Never suggest paid plans (ElevenLabs free tier forbids commercial use and requires credit; Azure needs a card).
